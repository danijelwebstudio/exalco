ABOUT EXALCO ALUMINIUM COMPANY

This company, with an area of 16,000 square meters and a 6,000-meter hall, operates in separate units, including Italian joining machines, decorative machines (wood effect), extrusion presses of 1,800 tons, OEN brand thermal-break profile joining, as well as a powder coating plant with automatic machines.

Company EXALCO, originally started by providing powder coating services, and in 2008 successfully launched an extrusion line for the production of various types of construction and industrial aluminum profiles. Currently, it produces and markets its products according to defined standards.
