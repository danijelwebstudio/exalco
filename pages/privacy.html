Privacy & Cookies

Information about how this website uses data and analytics cookies.

Last updated: September 25, 2026.

1. About this policy

This page explains what information may be processed when using the EXALCO Aluminium website and how analytics and related technologies are used.

2. Information processed by the website

This website does not provide user accounts or direct online purchasing. Visitors contact the company directly by telephone or email.

During normal use, the hosting server may automatically record technical information such as an IP address, access time, requested page, browser and device information for security and proper website operation.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website, including which pages are visited and how visitors navigate through the site.

Google Analytics is loaded only after the visitor explicitly selects “Accept” in the cookie notice.

If “Decline” is selected, Google Analytics is not loaded.

After consent, Google Analytics may use analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_....

4. Saving your cookie preference

Your analytics preference is stored locally in your browser under the name analyticsConsent.

This is used only so the website can remember whether analytics was accepted or declined.

5. Changing your cookie preference

You can change your choice at any time.

Change cookie settings

6. Google Maps

Some pages contain embedded Google Maps content. When the map is loaded, your browser may establish a connection with Google services and transmit technical information required to display the map.

7. Google Fonts and Font Awesome

The website uses Google Fonts and Font Awesome to display fonts and icons. Loading those resources may cause your browser to connect to servers operated by the relevant service providers.

8. Contact by email or telephone

If you contact us by email or telephone, information you voluntarily provide may be used to respond to your enquiry, communicate with you and manage a potential business relationship.

9. Sharing of information

We do not sell visitors' personal information.

Certain technical information may be processed by service providers used for website operation, including hosting infrastructure, Google Analytics, Google Maps, Google Fonts and CDN providers.

Depending on the laws applicable to you, you may have rights to request information about personal data relating to you, request correction or deletion, and withdraw previously given consent where processing is based on consent.
