EXALCO

This company, with an area of 16,000 square meters and a 6,000-meter hall, is operating in separate units equipped with modern technology.

We utilize Italian sewing machines, decorative machines (wood design), 1,800-ton extrusion machines, and thermal break profile sewing with the OEN brand, along with a powder unit equipped with automatic machines.

Kousar Zanjan Aluminum Company, with the brand name "EXALCO", initially started its work by providing powder services and in 2008 succeeded in launching an extrusion line to produce various types of construction and industrial aluminum sections.
